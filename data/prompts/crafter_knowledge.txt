# About Crafter
Crafter is a 2D open-world survival environment. Your goal is to maximize your survival time while manage your vital signs, gather resources, craft tools, and defend yourself against hostile creatures.

1. Vital Signs & Status
- Health: You start with 9/9 Health. You lose health when attacked by enemies or if your Food/Drink levels drop to zero. Health regenerates over time if your Food and Drink levels are high.
- Food: You start with 9/9 Food. It decreases over time. Replenish it by hunting cows or eating plants.
- Drink: You start with 9/9 Drink. It decreases over time. Replenish it by drinking from water tiles.
- Energy: Used for performing actions. Replenish it by sleeping.
- Inventory: You have an inventory to store gathered resources (Wood, Stone, Coal, Iron, Diamond, Saplings) and crafted items (Tools, Stations) up to 9 items each.

2. Basic Mechanics
- Grid World: The world is a 2D grid containing materials like Grass, Water, Stone, Sand, Path, Lava, and Trees.
- Movement: You can move Up, Down, Left, or Right (move_up, move_down, move_left, move_right).
- Interaction: Use the do action to interact with the object or material directly in front of you. This includes attacking, gathering, eating, or drinking.
- Sleeping: Use the sleep action to rest. This restores Energy and safely skips through the dangerous night, provided you are not in immediate danger.

3. Resource Gathering & Tool Hierarchy You need specific tools to harvest harder materials.
- Wood: Harvest Trees (Requires: None). Yields 1 Wood.
- Saplings: Harvest Grass (Requires: None). Low probability (10%) to yield 1 Sapling.
- Water: Interact with Water (Requires: None). Replenishes Drink level.
- Stone: Harvest Stone (Requires: Wood Pickaxe). Yields 1 Stone.
- Coal: Harvest Coal (Requires: Wood Pickaxe). Yields 1 Coal.
- Iron: Harvest Iron (Requires: Stone Pickaxe). Yields 1 Iron.
- Diamond: Harvest Diamond (Requires: Iron Pickaxe). Yields 1 Diamond.

4. Crafting & Stations Most advanced items require a crafting station to be placed nearby.
- Crafting Table:
    - Recipe: 2 Wood.
    - Usage: Required to craft Wood/Stone Pickaxes and Swords.
    - !IMPORTANT! Crafting Table cannot be stored in the inventory, as it is NOT an item.
- Furnace:
    - Recipe: 1 Stone.
    - Usage: Required (alongside a Table) to craft Iron Pickaxes and Swords.
    - !IMPORTANT! Furnace cannot be stored in the inventory, as it is NOT an item.
    - Tip: When furnace is required, placing both table and furnace is strongly recommended.
- Unlike Minecraft, table and furnace cannot be broken, you may need to frequently make them.

5. Tool & Weapon Recipes
- Wood Pickaxe: 1 Wood (Requires: Table).
- Stone Pickaxe: 1 Wood + 1 Stone (Requires: Table).
- Iron Pickaxe: 1 Wood + 1 Coal + 1 Iron (Requires: Table + Furnace).
- Wood Sword: 1 Wood (Requires: Table).
- Stone Sword: 1 Wood + 1 Stone (Requires: Table).
- Iron Sword: 1 Wood + 1 Coal + 1 Iron (Requires: Table + Furnace).

6. Combat & Enemies
- Zombies: Spawn on Grass, primarily at night. They chase and attack you.
- Skeletons: Spawn on Paths. They shoot arrows from a distance.
- Defense: You can attack enemies using the do action. Swords significantly increase your damage output compared to bare hands or pickaxes.
- Strategy: Nighttime is dangerous due to increased spawn rates. Building shelter or sleeping is recommended.

7. Farming & Building
- Place Plant: 1 Sapling. Grows into a mature plant over time and, after 300 steps, it can be eaten.
- Place Stone: 1 Stone. Creates a wall to block enemies.
- Cows: Passive mobs. Can be hunted for Food.

8. Achievements (Goals) Unlock these by performing the specific actions:
- Gathering: Collect Wood, Stone, Coal, Iron, Diamond, Drink, Sapling.
- Crafting: Make Wood/Stone/Iron Pickaxe, Make Wood/Stone/Iron Sword, Place Table, Place Furnace.
- Farming/Building: Place Plant, Place Stone, Eat Plant, Wake Up.
- Combat: Defeat Zombie, Defeat Skeleton.
- Survival: Eat Cow.
